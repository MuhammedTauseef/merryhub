📅 Advent Activity Cards

Sheet 1 of 5 — December 1–6 • Cut along the dashed lines. Draw one card each morning from 1 December — or hide them in an Advent calendar.

1December🎄

The countdown begins

Hang up the Advent calendar, put on the first Christmas song of the year, and tell everyone your Christmas wish — out loud, for luck.

☐ Done it!
2December💌

Card for a neighbour

Make a Christmas card for a neighbour or someone on your street, and deliver it by hand (posted through the letterbox counts).

☐ Done it!
3December☕

Hot chocolate ceremony

Hot chocolate with all the toppings you can find, plus a singalong to your family's favourite carol. Marshmallows are not optional.

☐ Done it!
4December❄️

Snowflake blizzard

Cut ten paper snowflakes — no two alike — and tape them to one window to make your own indoor blizzard.

☐ Done it!
5December🎁

The giving box

Fill a box with toys, books or clothes you've outgrown and give it to a charity shop or a family who'd love it.

☐ Done it!
6December🗣️

Three languages

Learn to say “Merry Christmas” in three other languages and teach them to the whole family at dinner.

📅 Advent Activity Cards

Sheet 2 of 5 — December 7–12 • Cut along the dashed lines. Draw one card each morning from 1 December — or hide them in an Advent calendar.

7December🍪

Biscuit day

Bake or decorate biscuits together. One must be shaped like a star. One must be eaten immediately, for quality control.

☐ Done it!
8December🔦

Lights walk

After dinner, wrap up warm and walk the neighbourhood on a Christmas-lights hunt. Award points for the twinkliest house.

☐ Done it!
9December🧝

Family of elves

Draw your whole family as Santa's elves — pointy ears, workshop jobs and all. Give everyone an official elf name.

☐ Done it!
10December✉️

A thank-you note

Write a real thank-you note to someone who helps you all year — a teacher, a postie, a grandparent — and send it today.

☐ Done it!
11December🧣

Blanket-fort cinema

Build a blanket fort, pick a Christmas story or film, and hold the screening inside. Tickets: one hug each.

☐ Done it!
12December🎶

Freeze dance

Christmas songs on, everyone dances; when the music stops, FREEZE. Last one wobbling is out — until the next song.

☐ Done it!

📅 Advent Activity Cards

Sheet 3 of 5 — December 13–18 • Cut along the dashed lines. Draw one card each morning from 1 December — or hide them in an Advent calendar.

13December💛

Secret kindness

Leave a kind note somewhere for someone in your family to find — in a shoe, a pocket or under a pillow.

☐ Done it!
14December🐦

Feed the birds

Make simple bird feeders (a pine cone, some peanut butter and seed works beautifully) and hang them where you can watch.

☐ Done it!
15December🎬

Movie night vote

Everyone nominates a Christmas film, the family votes, and the winner is watched with popcorn. Losers get to pick the snacks.

☐ Done it!
16December😂

Invent a joke

Invent a brand-new Christmas joke and test it at dinner. Groans count as applause. Write the best one down for next year.

☐ Done it!
17December🏷️

Tag team

Turn last year's Christmas cards into this year's gift tags — cut out the prettiest pictures and add string or ribbon.

☐ Done it!
18December⭐

Wish on a star

Find the brightest star in the evening sky and let everyone make one quiet wish. No telling — wishes are shy.

☐ Done it!

📅 Advent Activity Cards

Sheet 4 of 5 — December 19–24 • Cut along the dashed lines. Draw one card each morning from 1 December — or hide them in an Advent calendar.

19December🙌

Wrap star

Help wrap a present properly — holding the paper, mastering the tape, and signing the tag in your best handwriting.

☐ Done it!
20December📞

Carol call

Sing a carol to grandparents or far-away family over the phone or a video call. Costumes encouraged, rehearsal optional.

☐ Done it!
21December🏠

Gingerbread HQ

Build a gingerbread house — real biscuits or the cardboard-and-glue edition. Every headquarters needs a biscuit door.

☐ Done it!
22December🎅

Floor angel

No snow? No problem. Lie on a rug and make a “floor angel”, or draw one in chalk outside. Photograph the evidence.

☐ Done it!
23December🕯️

The fancy table

Set tonight's table like a celebration: candles (grown-ups light them), the good glasses, and everyone's name in place.

☐ Done it!
24December🎁

Santa's snack squad

Prepare Santa's snack plate and a note for the reindeer. Then: pyjamas, teeth, bed — an early night is part of the magic.

☐ Done it!
